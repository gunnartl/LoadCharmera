Charmera Import

Kodak Charmera → iPhone

  1. Connect the Kodak Charmera to the iPhone with its USB-C cable.
  2. Tap the button below and choose Choose Files.
  3. Tap Browse, open the camera (CR1KX_DATA) and go into DCIM.
  4. Tap ⋯ → Select, mark the photos and videos you want (or Select All), then Open.
Everything happens on this phone. Nothing is uploaded. Keep this page open while videos convert.
